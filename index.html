BookTime Studio

A private production tool operated by the owner of the Booktime Stories Studio YouTube channel. It organizes original literary commentary, local narration, rendered media, episode reviews and owner-authorized uploads.

The Google integration identifies the connected YouTube channel, checks video status and uploads approved video files. Public release requires a separate decision by the channel owner. This tool is not offered for connecting other people's accounts.

Videos are published on Booktime Stories Studio on YouTube. This informational site contains no sign-in form and does not expose the production server.
